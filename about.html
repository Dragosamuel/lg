About Lemon Gaba SDA Choir

Founded in 2018, the Lemongaba SDA Choir began with a divine mission to share the love and hope of Jesus Christ through music.

2018

The Beginning

Started as Grace Kids Choir under Sister Grace Jimmy's leadership.

2019

Official Formation

Became Lemongaba SDA Church Choir with Daniel Mark and Grace Jimmy leading 20 members.

2021

Post-Pandemic Growth

Expanded to 28 members under Stephine Charles and Eriga Rechard.

2022

First Album Release

Released debut album with eight inspiring songs, directed by Eriga Rechard and Monica Minialla.
